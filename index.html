AIFromZeroToHero Uploader

A personal tool for one YouTube channel owner.

AIFromZeroToHero Uploader is a small private tool used by the owner of the AI From Zero to Hero YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos.

What it does

After the owner has reviewed and approved a finished video, the tool uploads it to the owner's own channel through the YouTube Data API, together with its title and description. It requests a single permission: the ability to upload videos to the channel the owner signs in with.

What it is not

It is not a public service. It has no sign-ups, no other users, and it does not collect information from anyone who watches or visits the channel.
